Frequently Asked Questions

How can I rent furniture?

Open the Furniture page, select the furniture you want and click Add to Cart. Then open your Cart and click Rent Now.

Can I change the quantity?

Yes. You can increase or decrease the quantity directly from your Cart.

How can I track my order?

After your rental is confirmed, use your Order ID on the Track Order page.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes. Open the Track Order page and enter your Order ID. If cancellation is available, you can cancel the order there.

What payment methods are available?

FurniRent provides UPI, Debit/Credit Card and Cash on Delivery options in the payment section.

Do I need an account to rent furniture?

Yes. You need to log in or create an account before using the FurniRent rental service.
